# Enterprise Telemetry Attribute Dictionary & Business Value Mapping

This document provides a comprehensive inventory of all **8 operational and master metadata datasets** generated by the HydroGrow Smart Farming Platform. 

It maps every physical attribute to its operational meaning, business value, persona consumers, derived KPIs, and supported analytics capabilities.

---

## 1. `EquipmentTelemetry` (High-Frequency Mechanical Telemetry — 10s Sampling Cadence)

### Table Schema & Attribute Dictionary

| Attribute | Physical Representation | Business Value & Operational Impact | Target Consumer Persona | Derived KPIs & Metrics | Analytics Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `event_id` | Unique UUID event string | Guarantees exact-once deduplication across Spark streaming pipelines. | Data Engineer | Ingestion Deduplication Ratio | Pipeline Data Integrity |
| `event_type` | Stream classification (`equipment_telemetry`) | Routes payload to correct landing tables and Eventstream topics. | DataOps Lead | Stream Volume by Type | Ingress Routing & Auditing |
| `facility_id` | Philippine facility code (e.g. `FAC-001`) | Core dimensional key for cross-facility performance benchmarking. | VP Operations, Regional Lead | Facility Operational Health Score | Cross-Facility Comparison |
| `zone_id` | Hydroponic growing room (e.g. `ZONE-003`) | Pinpoints exact growing rack location experiencing machinery fault. | Farm Manager, Field Tech | Zone Degradation Index | Spatial Heatmap Visualization |
| `equipment_id` | Asset tracking code (e.g. `EQ-00054`) | Granular asset-level tracking for maintenance history and warranty claims. | Maintenance Supervisor | Equipment Failure Count | Asset Lifetime Tracking |
| `installation_date` | Equipment commissioning date (`2025-01-15` default baseline) | Baseline commissioning timestamp for calculating total machinery asset age and lifespan wear. | Asset Manager, Operations Lead | Asset Age (Days), Lifespan Wear Rate | Depreciation & Capital Expenditure Planning |
| `equipment_type` | Equipment category (Water Pump, HVAC, LED) | Enables equipment class reliability comparisons and vendor audits. | Procurement Lead, Maintenance Manager | MTBF by Equipment Type | Asset Reliability Benchmarking |

| `manufacturer` / `model_number` | Manufacturer name & hardware model code | Evaluates supplier quality and component failure rates. | Vendor Management Lead | Vendor Failure Rate ($\%$) | Supplier QA & Warranty Audit |
| `operating_status` | Status code (`RUNNING`, `DEGRADED`, `CRITICAL_FAILURE`) | Real-time visibility into machine availability and operational downtime. | Maintenance Lead, Operations Manager | Equipment Availability ($\%$), Downtime Hours | Real-Time Dashboard & Alerting |
| `health` | Normalized mechanical health percentage ($0.0\% - 100.0\%$) | Instant indicator of machine wear and operational degradation. | Reliability Engineer | Mean Equipment Health Score | Anomaly Detection, Trend Analysis |
| `current_load` | Electrical/mechanical load percentage ($0.0\% - 100.0\%$) | Identifies over-worked pumps or HVAC compressors operating near peak load. | Energy Steward, Maintenance Supervisor | Peak Load Ratio ($\%$), Overload Frequency | Predictive Maintenance, Load Balancing |
| `failure_probability` | ML-driven component breakdown probability ($0.00 - 1.00$) | Predicts catastrophic pump/HVAC failure before physical breakdown occurs. | Maintenance Supervisor, DataOps Lead | Failure Risk Score, MTBF | **Predictive Analytics**, Pre-emptive Work Orders |
| `runtime_hours` | Cumulative operating hours | Tracks component lifecycle to schedule preventative maintenance servicing. | Maintenance Lead, Asset Manager | Total Operating Hours, Service Deficit | Preventative Maintenance Scheduling |
| `vibration_vps` | Bearing & motor vibration amplitude ($VPS$) | Primary physical indicator of mechanical imbalance, cavitation, or shaft wear. | Reliability Engineer, Mechanical Specialist | Vibration Anomaly Count, Bearing Wear Severity | **Vibration Spectrum Anomaly Detection** |
| `operating_temperature_c` | Motor casing operating temperature ($^\circ C$) | Detects thermal overheating caused by electrical overload or bearing friction. | Reliability Engineer, HVAC Specialist | Overheating Incident Count, Thermal Stress Index | Thermal Drift Anomaly Detection |
| `power_consumption_kw` | Real-time active power draw ($kW$) | Direct input for facility energy billing and power grid SLA monitoring. | Energy Steward, CFO | Total Power Draw ($kW$), Energy Intensity per $m^2$ | Energy Forecasting, Utility Cost Allocation |
| `operator_contact` / `phone` | On-duty technician name & emergency hotline | Enables automated direct voice/SMS dispatch during emergency failures. | Dispatch Lead, Field Tech | Response Latency | Emergency Escalation Routing |
| `timestamp` | UTC payload event timestamp | Aligns equipment fault events with environmental micro-climate anomalies. | DataOps Lead, Reliability Engineer | Incident Time-to-Detect ($TTD$) | Cross-Stream Event Correlation |
